Privacy Policy

Projiq ("we", "our", or "us") operates the project management platform at projiq.app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — written in plain English, not legalese.

Overview

Projiq is a cloud-based project management tool used by engineering and operations teams. When you register an organization, invite teammates, and manage projects, we process data on your behalf. We act as a data processor for content you create inside Projiq, and as a data controller for account and billing information.

Short version: We collect only what we need to run the service, we do not sell your data to anyone, and you can delete your account and data at any time.

Information We Collect

Account & Registration Data
  • Full name and work email address
  • Organization name, industry, and team size
  • Password (we never store your plaintext password — it is hashed before saving)
  • Google account profile (if you sign in with Google)
  • Subscription plan and billing country
Content You Create
  • Projects, issues, tasks, comments, and attachments
  • Sprint boards, Kanban columns, and workflow configurations
  • Team member assignments and activity history
Usage & Technical Data
  • IP address, browser type, and operating system
  • Pages visited, features used, and session duration
  • Error logs and performance metrics
  • Timestamps for logins, project actions, and API calls
Payment Data

Payments are processed through Razorpay. We receive a transaction ID and subscription status but do not store your full card number or CVV. Razorpay's own privacy policy governs how they handle payment details.

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Infrastructure and tooling partners (cloud hosting, email delivery, payment processing) who are contractually bound to process data only on our instructions.
  • Your organization: Admins in your Projiq organization can see member profiles, activity, and content within that organization.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of Projiq and its users.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred to the successor entity, subject to the same privacy commitments.

Storage & Security

Your data is stored on AWS infrastructure. We apply industry-standard safeguards:

  • All data in transit is protected using industry-standard transport encryption
  • Passwords are processed with a one-way cryptographic hash — we cannot reverse or read them
  • Authentication sessions use signed, time-limited tokens that expire automatically
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel with MFA
  • Regular automated backups are retained for 30 days

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We maintain reasonable safeguards and will notify affected users promptly if a breach occurs.

Cookies & Tracking

Projiq uses a small number of cookies and local storage entries:

  • Session token: Required to keep you signed in. Expires when you log out or after 30 days of inactivity.
  • Preferences: Theme (dark/light) and UI settings stored locally in your browser.
  • CSRF protection: A short-lived token to prevent cross-site request forgery.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels on the authenticated application. The marketing landing page at projiq.app may use basic analytics to measure page visits.

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information from your account settings or by contacting us.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated personal data. Content shared within your organization may be subject to your organization admin's retention settings.
  • Portability: Export your projects and issues as JSON or CSV from within the app.
  • Objection: Opt out of non-essential communications from your account notification settings.
  • Restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any right, email us at privacy@projiq.app. We will respond within 30 days.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service:

  • Account data: Retained until account deletion is requested. Deleted within 30 days of request.
  • Project content: Retained while the organization subscription is active. Deleted 90 days after subscription cancellation.
  • Billing records: Retained for 7 years to meet financial and tax reporting requirements.
  • Server logs: Retained for 90 days for security and debugging, then deleted.
  • Backups: Rolling 30-day backup window; data deleted from a backup after that backup expires.

Children's Privacy

Projiq is a professional business tool intended for use by adults in a workplace context. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 16. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will promptly delete the account.

Changes to This Policy

When we make material changes, we will:

  1. Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page
  2. Send an email notification to the admin of each active organization
  3. Display an in-app notice for 14 days after the change takes effect

Continued use of Projiq after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
